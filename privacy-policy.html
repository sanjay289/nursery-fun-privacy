Nursery Fun – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026
App: Nursery Fun (package com.confidentor.nurseryfun)

Nursery Fun is a learning app for nursery and pre-school children. We take children's privacy very seriously. This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or any other personal data. It does not have accounts or sign-in.

2. Internet and permissions

The app does not request the Internet permission or any other Android permission. All content (letters, numbers, rhymes, pictures, music) is built into the app and works fully offline.

3. Ads, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases, and no links to websites or social media. No data is sent to us or to any third party.

4. Data stored on the device

The app saves only three small settings on the device itself: the star score, the chosen language (English/Hindi), and whether sound is on or off. These never leave the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

5. Text-to-speech

To read words aloud, the app uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device (for example, Google Speech Services). Only the built-in learning words are spoken; no user input is sent.

6. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it is designed to comply with Google Play's Families Policy, COPPA (USA), GDPR-K (EU), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
