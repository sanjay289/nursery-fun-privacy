Khel Khel Mein (खेल खेल में) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026
App: खेल खेल में: Learning Games (package com.msratanpur.khelkhelmein)

Developer: Sanjay kumar singh (Headmaster, Middle School Ratanpur, Begusarai, Bihar, India)

Khel Khel Mein is a learning-games app for children of Class 1 to 8, with games for maths, times tables, words, spelling, alphabet, general knowledge and science, in Hindi and English. We take children's privacy very seriously. This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts, or any other personal data. It has no accounts and no sign-in.

2. Internet and permissions

The app does not use the Internet. All games and questions are built into the app and work fully offline. The only Android permission is vibrate, used for a short vibration when an answer is wrong.

3. Ads, analytics and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases, and no links to websites or social media. No data is sent to us or to any third party.

4. Data stored on the device

The app saves only a few game settings on the device itself: coins, stars, best scores, number of games played, and the chosen language (Hindi/English). These never leave the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

5. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it is designed to comply with Google Play's Families Policy, COPPA (USA), GDPR-K (EU), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

6. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
